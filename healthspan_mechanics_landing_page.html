Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Isn't barbell lifting dangerous for joint health or over-50s?
A: Unsupervised lifting is risky. Supervised, progressive barbell loading guided by longevity-trained coaches is the safest, most effective way to strengthen joints and surrounding muscle.
Q: What if I have never touched a barbell before in my life?
A: Perfect. This course is explicitly designed for beginners, starting with unweighted wooden dowels or light technique bars to master mechanics.
Q: How does the DEXA scan work?
A: You receive a non-invasive, 6-minute body composition scan at week 1 and week 8, giving you precise medical-grade data on bone density, muscle mass and fat distribution.
Q: Can I join if I have existing osteopenia or minor joint stiffness?
A: Yes. All movements are regressed or modified to suit individual mobility limitations.
