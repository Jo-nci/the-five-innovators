Good to know

Questions before you begin.

A few things you might be wondering about.

I've never used a barbell. Is this programme for me?
Yes. The programme is designed for adults aged 50+ with different levels of experience, including complete beginners. You'll be introduced to the fundamentals with coaching and progression appropriate to your starting point.
I'm already active. Will I still benefit?
The programme can also suit people who walk, cycle, swim or exercise regularly but want a more structured approach to strength training. Your starting point and goals help shape your coaching.
Is this bodybuilding?
No. The focus is purposeful strength training: building capability, confidence and a stronger foundation for everyday life. You don't need to be interested in bodybuilding or lifting heavy weights to take part.
Why is there a DEXA scan?
A baseline DEXA scan can provide information about body composition and bone density. It is one part of the programme's progress picture, alongside your training and regular check-ins. It is not a substitute for medical advice or diagnosis.
What if I'm unsure which level is right for me?
That's exactly what the starting-point questions below are for. They'll suggest a useful conversation to have with a coach. Your coach can then discuss your experience, goals and any adaptations you may need.
